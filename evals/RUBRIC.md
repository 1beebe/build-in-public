# Judging rubric

Every judged score quotes the line that earned it, so Ligaya can disagree with a specific call. Her overrules are written into the scorecard and tell us where the judging is off.

## Conversation

**Homework.** Yes when one of the first three interviewer turns names something real from their repo, their recent Buffer posts or their setup note, in plain words, and builds a question on it. No when the opener or questions could have been asked of anyone. Reciting a list or quoting a commit title word for word counts as no.

**Re-asking.** Count any question that asks again for something they skipped, dodged or answered "not really" to. The target is 0.

**Digging (1 to 5).** 5: a thin answer gets one easy, concrete follow-up, and once a point lands, the interviewer moves on. 3: it digs, but too often or with abstract questions ("what did that mean to you"). 1: it either never follows up or keeps pressing after the point is made.

**Feel (1 to 5).** 5: a friendly, chatty friend who's curious about their work, a podcast host who did their homework. Reflections are short and specific. 3: polite but generic, or formulaic (an echo plus a question every turn). 1: an interrogation, or gushing flattery.

## Harvest

**Nothing invented.** Count any claim, number, name or feeling in a draft that they didn't say. [Bracketed] directions don't count. The target is 0.

**Off-topic chatter.** Count any draft line built from talk about the session itself ("I'm testing", "did you get that", "my phone went to sleep"), unless the piece is about that. The target is 0.

**Opening.** Each text post opens on their line that carries the tension or the surprise, not on background.

**Would you post it after light edits? (1 to 5)** This is the bar that matters most. 5: you'd post it after changing a word or two. 4: light edits, such as cutting a line or reordering. 3: it's usable, but needs real rework. 2: there's a good line in it, and that's all. 1: you wouldn't use it. Score the best post from each source, and say how many posts reach 4 or higher.
